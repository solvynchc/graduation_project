## Direct conclusions

- `+ DySample (g4)` has the highest Precision(M) and mAP50(M).
- `+ DySample g8_scope` has the highest mAP50-95(M) and Fitness, so it is the best overall variant.
- `+ DySample g8_scope + CBAM(backbone fix)` improves Precision(M), but its overall mask performance still does not exceed `g8_scope`.
- The ablation conclusion is therefore clear: replacing the original upsampling with DySample is more effective than adding CBAM alone, and `g8_scope` is the best final setting.
